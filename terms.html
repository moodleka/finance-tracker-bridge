Terms of Use — Finance Tracker

Last updated: 2026-09-04

Finance Tracker is a personal, single-user application built and operated solely by its developer for their own private use. It is not published, sold, or made available to the public, and has no other end users or accounts besides the developer's own.

Purpose

The app reads the developer's own bank account balances and transactions, via the Enable Banking API, to consolidate accounts held in different countries into one personal view.

No warranty

The app is provided as-is, for personal use only, with no warranty of any kind. It is used at the developer's own risk and is not a substitute for official bank statements.

Access to bank data

Access to the developer's bank accounts is granted directly by the developer through their bank's own authentication and consent flow (Strong Customer Authentication), via Enable Banking as a licensed account information service provider. This access can be revoked at any time via the developer's bank or Enable Banking.
