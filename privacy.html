Privacy Policy — Finance Tracker

Last updated: 2026-09-04

Finance Tracker is a personal, single-user iOS application built and used only by its developer to view their own bank accounts in one place. It is not distributed publicly and does not have other end users.

What data is accessed

The app connects to the developer's own bank accounts via the Enable Banking API (an authorized PSD2 account information service provider) to read account balances and transaction history. No other personal data is collected.

Where data is stored

All data is stored locally on the developer's own device: API credentials in the iOS Keychain, and account/transaction data in local app storage. Nothing is sent to any server other than the Enable Banking API and the developer's own banks to fetch this data.

Data sharing

No data is shared with, sold to, or processed by any third party other than Enable Banking, which acts solely as the licensed intermediary to the developer's own banks.

Data retention and deletion

Data persists only on the developer's device and can be deleted at any time by removing a linked account in the app or deleting the app itself.
